# VEDA Task 23 – SQL Window Functions: Business Insights

1. **Product Ranking:** RANK() and DENSE_RANK() help compare product prices and identify high-priced products.

2. **Order Sequence:** ROW_NUMBER() assigns a unique sequential number to orders or products, making records easier to organize.

3. **Previous and Next Orders:** LAG() and LEAD() help compare order dates and understand the time between orders.

4. **Running Sales Total:** SUM() OVER() calculates cumulative order values and helps track sales growth across orders.

5. **Average Order Value:** AVG() OVER() helps compare individual order values with the overall average.

6. **Customer Sales Analysis:** PARTITION BY customer_id calculates customer-wise sales totals and helps compare customer contributions.

7. **Latest Orders:** Ranking orders within each customer group helps identify their most recent orders.

8. **Customer Order Comparison:** Comparing an order's value with the customer's average order value helps identify relatively higher- and lower-value orders.

## Conclusion

SQL window functions make it possible to analyze rankings, sales totals, averages, and order sequences without losing individual row-level details.
